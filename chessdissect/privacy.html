ChessDissect Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

ChessDissect is a chess analysis app for Android developed by Archnimzo. It works offline most of the time and needs no account. This policy explains what information leaves your device and where it goes.

Summary

  • There are no user accounts and no sign-in.
  • There is no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • We run no servers: the developer does not receive or store any of your data.
  • Your games, analyses, mistakes and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The app only connects to the internet for the features described below, always directly to the relevant service (Lichess, Chess.com or GitHub).

Data stored on your device

Games you import or download, their analyses, the mistakes the app detects and your preferences (board, pieces, engine options) are kept in the app's internal storage. Analysis is done by the Stockfish engine on the device itself. None of this is sent anywhere.

Only when you ask for it does the app export files (PGN or backups) to your Downloads folder or a location you choose, copy text to the clipboard, or open the Android share menu.

If Android backup to your Google account is turned on, the system may include the app's data in that backup. It is handled by Google according to your settings; the developer has no access to it.

You can delete all of this data from within the app or by uninstalling it (or with "Clear data" in Android settings).

Their use of that data is governed by their own policies: Lichess, Chess.com and GitHub. All connections use HTTPS.

Android permissions

  • Internet: for the connections in the table above.
  • Network state: to warn you before downloading the NNUE network without Wi-Fi.

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Children

ChessDissect does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes what it does with data, we will update this page and its effective date.
